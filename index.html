ABOUT THE SPORT

NOT JUST
A DISC.

Ultimate Frisbee is one of the fastest-growing sports in the world, combining speed, strategy, teamwork and athleticism. What truly makes it unique is the Spirit of the Game — a culture of honesty, respect and self-officiation where players take responsibility for fair play without referees.

⚡ Fast

High-speed gameplay with continuous movement, quick decisions and explosive plays.

🤝 Spirit

Respect, honesty and sportsmanship define every match.

🏃 Athletic

Sprint, jump, dive and throw. Ultimate rewards all-round athleticism.

🌍 Global

Played competitively in over 100 countries around the world.

🥏 Self-Officiated

Players make their own calls, making Spirit of the Game one of Ultimate's most unique and respected traditions.

THE BASICS

01

Seven players compete on each team.

02

Players cannot run while holding the disc.

03

Catch the disc inside the opponent's end zone to score.

04

Any drop, interception or out-of-bounds throw results in a turnover.

05

Ultimate is self-officiated and guided by the Spirit of the Game.

OUR STORY

FROM A SINGLE DISC
TO A GROWING COMMUNITY.

Founded in 2018, the Ultimate Frisbee Club at IIT Kanpur has grown from a small group of enthusiasts into one of the institute's most active sporting communities. Today, IIT Kanpur proudly stands among the few IITs with a dedicated Ultimate Frisbee team, competing, hosting tournaments, and introducing hundreds of students to the sport every year.
